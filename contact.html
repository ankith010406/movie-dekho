About MovieDekoo

MovieDekoo.com lets you find movies, pick seats and get your ticket in a few taps.

Common questions

How do I book a ticket?

Open a movie, choose a date and time, tap your seats, enter your details and pay.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Go to My bookings, find your booking with your email or ID, and select Cancel.

Is the payment real?

No. This is a college project, so payments are simulated.

How many seats can I book at once?

Up to 8 seats per booking.
